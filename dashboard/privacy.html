KAAPAV ARC Studios Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

KAAPAV ARC Studios uses a private automation application to publish and measure original studio content on accounts owned and controlled by KAAPAV. The application is not offered as a public login service and does not sell personal information.

Data processed

The application processes identifiers for the KAAPAV Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account, publishing permissions, content metadata, media identifiers, comments and aggregated performance insights returned by Meta.

Purpose and retention

Data is used only for authenticated publishing, release recovery, duplicate prevention, moderation and evidence-based performance reporting. Access tokens are stored locally in an excluded credential store. Operational records are retained only as needed for continuity, audit and security.

Sharing

Studio data is not sold. It is shared only with service providers required to operate Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and the secured KAAPAV control infrastructure.

Requests

For access or deletion requests, use the data deletion page. This policy may be updated when platform requirements or studio operations change.
