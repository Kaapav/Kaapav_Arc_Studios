Data Deletion Instructions

KAAPAV ARC Studios operates this application only for studio-controlled business accounts.

To request deletion of data associated with an authorized Meta account, contact the KAAPAV ARC Studios Page through its official Facebook or Instagram messaging channel and include the reference KAAPAV-DATA-DELETION. After ownership verification, locally retained account identifiers, analytics snapshots and publishing records will be deleted unless retention is required for security, legal compliance or duplicate-release protection.

Revoking the application from Meta Business Integrations immediately stops future API access. Local access tokens can also be removed from the secured KAAPAV credential store.
