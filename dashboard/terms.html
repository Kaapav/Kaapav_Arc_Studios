KAAPAV ARC Studios Terms

Last updated: 17 August 2026

The KAAPAV ARC Studios automation application is a private studio tool used to manage original content on KAAPAV-controlled accounts. It is provided without public enrollment or third-party account management.

Authorized use

Only the studio owner and explicitly authorized operators may access the application. Users must comply with Meta, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and applicable laws.

Content and availability

KAAPAV retains rights in its original stories, characters, artwork and productions. Platform availability, distribution and analytics are controlled partly by external services and cannot be guaranteed.

Security

Attempts to bypass authentication, retrieve credentials, duplicate releases or misuse publishing permissions are prohibited.
