Drop a royalty-free background track here named "music.mp3" to have it mixed
under the narration at low volume (controlled by video.music_volume in config.yaml).

Free, safe-for-YouTube sources:
  - YouTube Audio Library (studio.youtube.com -> Audio Library)
  - Pixabay Music (pixabay.com/music) — CC0
  - Uppbeat / Bensound (check the license for Shorts)

If no music.mp3 is present, videos are simply narration-only. That's fine.
