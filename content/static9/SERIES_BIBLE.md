# STATIC//9 — Series Bible

**Genre:** science-fiction survival thriller
**Premise:** After a violet solar storm kills every network in flooded Navastra City, sixteen-year-old radio builder Isha Rao discovers that abandoned emergency Channel 9 announces a person's name exactly nine minutes before the city takes them.
**Season engine:** Every episode solves an immediate survival problem while exposing another layer of Channel 9, the signal-borne Choir, and Isha's missing mother's role in sealing District Nine.
**Ending promise:** The survivors must decide whether restoring the world is worth releasing a signal that can wear human memories like bodies.

## Non-negotiable visual identity

Vertical 9:16 premium cute cinematic 3D feature-animation, original compact stylized characters with large expressive eyes and realistic cloth and wet-surface detail, storm-dark teal and electric violet palette, dramatic volumetric light, emotionally truthful acting, coherent anatomy and environments; suspenseful rather than graphic; preserve all locked character sheets exactly.

## Locked cast

### Isha Rao

- Role: sixteen-year-old radio builder and reluctant leader
- Permanent design lock: small Indian teen girl, warm brown skin, huge dark-brown eyes, blunt chin-length black bob with one silver lightning-shaped hair clip, mustard raincoat over charcoal hoodie, teal cargo trousers, red canvas shoes, copper radio-tool bracelet, battered rectangular analog receiver with orange dial
- Acting rule: thinks with her hands; bravery appears as controlled breathing, never swagger

### Kabir Rao

- Role: Isha's clever eleven-year-old brother
- Permanent design lock: tiny Indian boy, warm brown skin, round cheeks, large hazel-brown eyes, messy black curls, cobalt puffer vest over striped cream shirt, rust shorts over black leggings, yellow sneakers, homemade hearing protectors around neck
- Acting rule: uses jokes to hide fear; watches Isha before making dangerous choices

### Veer Malik

- Role: seventeen-year-old rooftop courier and principled rival
- Permanent design lock: lean Indian teen boy, deep brown skin, amber eyes, short wavy black hair shaved at sides, maroon waterproof bomber, graphite climbing harness, black utility trousers, white high-top shoes, collapsible magnetic grappling baton
- Acting rule: moves before speaking, then quietly checks who was left behind

### NIX

- Role: palm-sized repair drone carrying a fragment of Channel 9
- Permanent design lock: cute palm-sized spherical white-and-copper maintenance drone, two cyan oval eyes on black faceplate, three folding insect-like tool arms, small chipped numeral nine embossed on shell, no mouth
- Acting rule: eye shapes and hovering rhythm communicate emotion; never behaves like a pet

### Dr Meera Rao

- Role: Isha's missing mother and architect of the city signal firewall
- Permanent design lock: Indian woman early forties, warm brown skin, tired intelligent dark eyes, long black hair braided with silver thread, deep green storm coat over ivory technical suit, copper neural key at throat, scar through left eyebrow
- Acting rule: protective love expressed through precise choices, not speeches

### Warden Kade

- Role: commander of the sealed District Nine refuge
- Permanent design lock: broad Indian man late forties, medium brown skin, severe grey eyes, close-cropped salt-and-pepper hair, dark navy emergency armor with blank shoulder patch, red insulated gloves, mechanical respirator hanging at chest
- Acting rule: never raises his voice; genuine fear of chaos makes his cruelty believable

### Luma Sen

- Role: thirteen-year-old deaf mapmaker who reads the signal as vibration
- Permanent design lock: slender Indian girl, golden-brown skin, wide green-brown eyes, long dark hair in two low braids, lavender waterproof poncho, black leggings, mint boots, luminous haptic gloves and transparent vibration tablet
- Acting rule: direct eye contact, economical gestures, delight when patterns become visible

## Season episode spine

01. **The Radio Said His Name** — Channel 9 names Kabir, and Isha has nine minutes to learn what the warning really means.
02. **She Had Nine Minutes to Disappear** — Isha races the countdown and discovers that Channel 9 predicts attention, not death.
03. **Nine Witnesses Opened the Wall** — A rescue attempt gives the signal exactly nine witnesses and opens a doorway in concrete.
04. **Their Mother Was Waiting Underground** — Isha follows Meera into a station erased from city maps, unsure whether she is real.
05. **The Train Carried Nine Ishas** — Copies of Isha force Kabir to identify his sister by a memory the signal cannot steal.
06. **The Safe Zone Was a Listening Trap** — The group reaches Kade's refuge and discovers its walls feed every conversation to Channel 9.
07. **The Girl Who Could See Static** — Luma turns radio vibration into a map and exposes the moving heart of Channel 9.
08. **Kade Fed the City Its Memories** — Kade reveals why he sacrifices memories, forcing Isha to choose between truth and a living shelter.
09. **The Blackout Chose a Voice** — Without its memory feed, the signal takes control of a survivor and demands NIX.
10. **Sunrise Would Delete Their Mother** — Isha reaches Meera's last physical location and finds a choice hidden inside NIX.
11. **They Entered a Dead Broadcast** — Isha tunes the group into Channel 9's memory world, where the city repeats its final nine minutes.
12. **Veer Forgot Why He Came** — A memory loop strips Veer's identity, and Isha must restore him without inventing a false version.
13. **Veer Delivered the End of the City** — Veer confronts evidence that his last courier job carried the Choir into Tower Nine.
14. **Kabir Let the Signal Take Him** — Outside the signal, Kabir sacrifices his safety to open Tower Nine's route for the others.
15. **His Shadow Knew Their Future** — Kabir's stolen shadow reveals a future where Isha becomes the Choir's final voice.
16. **NIX Hid Their Mother's Last Memory** — NIX's secret memory proves Meera planned to erase him after the crisis.
17. **Their Mother Was Holding Back Nine Voices** — The team finds Meera imprisoning the Choir inside her own memories, close to collapse.
18. **The Voices Offered Isha Her Father** — The Choir tempts Isha with a flawless copy of her dead father while her nine-minute limit drains away.
19. **One Question Broke the Choir** — Luma weaponizes uncertainty, but breaking the Choir also destabilizes every stolen memory.
20. **The Lights Came Back Wrong** — Power returns across Navastra, but every illuminated device begins choosing one person to imitate.
21. **Tower Nine Invited Only Isha** — The glowing road rejects the team and offers Isha a private bargain at Tower Nine.
22. **Nine Futures Voted for Her** — Future copies pressure Isha to surrender control, each claiming their timeline saved more lives.
23. **This Was the Ninth Version of the City** — Evidence of eight erased timelines turns Kade's oldest secret into the key to stopping reset nine.
24. **Kabir Met the Brother Who Survived** — A reset copy offers Kabir a life without loss if he abandons the current city.
25. **Meera Could Save Only One Version** — The tower reconstructs eight Meeras, and NIX must decide which consciousness deserves a body.
26. **Ninety Seconds Before Everyone Changed** — The separated team races to sever nine reset cables before the countdown reaches zero.
27. **The Final Cable Was Isha** — Isha learns that cutting the last cable will erase her from every version of the city.
28. **The City Remembered Her Back** — Shared memories stabilize Isha, while Hunger makes one final attempt to turn gratitude into ownership.
29. **Channel 9 Was Calling Home** — The meteor receiver reveals the Choir's true origin and a threat still approaching Earth.
30. **They Kept Channel 9 Alive** — Isha must choose whether to destroy Channel 9 or preserve a dangerous line to the future.
