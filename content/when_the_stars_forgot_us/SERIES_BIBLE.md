# WHEN THE STARS FORGOT US — Series Bible

**Genre:** cosmic mystery drama
**Premise:** When every star vanishes from Earth's sky, fifteen-year-old Anvi Mehta receives a transmission from her missing astronaut brother saying the universe no longer remembers humanity exists.
**Season engine:** Each recovered point of starlight restores one erased relationship while revealing why Earth's Lighthouse Array deliberately removed humanity from the cosmic record.
**Ending promise:** Anvi must make humanity visible again without broadcasting private lives, sacrificing her brother, or inviting the danger the Array was built to hide from.
