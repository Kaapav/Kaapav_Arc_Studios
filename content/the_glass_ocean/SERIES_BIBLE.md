# THE GLASS OCEAN — Series Bible

**Genre:** mythic adventure
**Premise:** Eighteen years after the ocean turned to glass, fifteen-year-old salvage skater Nila Varma hears her drowned brother calling from beneath the transparent surface.
**Season engine:** Each fracture opens one buried sea-memory and one survival consequence for the coast, revealing who froze the ocean and why it may not survive a sudden release.
**Ending promise:** Nila must free the living sea without drowning the civilization built on its prison.

## Non-negotiable visual identity

Vertical 9:16 premium cute cinematic 3D feature-animation, original compact stylized characters with large expressive eyes, vast translucent glass ocean, coral light beneath the surface, wind-swept South Asian coastal settlements, tactile cloth, salt and crystal, mythic scale with emotionally truthful acting; preserve locked character sheets exactly; no graphic harm.

## Locked cast

### Nila Varma

- Role: fifteen-year-old glass-ocean salvage skater
- Permanent design lock: small dark-brown-skinned Indian teen girl, huge sea-green eyes, oval face, wind-tangled black hair in two short braids, coral-red sailcloth jacket, teal tunic, charcoal trousers, silver blade-skates, shell compass at throat
- Acting rule: physical confidence masks grief; pauses to read risk before committing

### Tavi

- Role: ten-year-old cartographer and Nila's cousin
- Permanent design lock: tiny brown-skinned Indian boy, round face, large amber eyes, fluffy black curls, oversized sky-blue goggles, cream poncho, mustard shorts over leggings, green boots, folding kite-map backpack
- Acting rule: turns fear into questions; corrects adults without performing cleverness

### Oru

- Role: young sea-spirit separated from the trapped ocean
- Permanent design lock: cute otter-sized translucent water creature, rounded fins, two luminous cyan eyes, coral-pink glow at heart, flowing tail suspended inside a clear droplet body, no mouth
- Acting rule: emotion through water shape, bubbles and heart-light; never pet-like

### Arun Varma

- Role: Nila's older brother preserved beneath the glass
- Permanent design lock: Indian young man age twenty-two, deep brown skin, gentle green-brown eyes, wavy black hair floating underwater, faded saffron rescue vest, navy shirt, shell bracelet matching Nila
- Acting rule: protective without directing Nila's choices; carries eighteen years of isolation

### Captain Mira Varma

- Role: Nila's mother and leader of the surface caravans
- Permanent design lock: strong Indian woman early forties, dark brown skin, sharp sea-green eyes, long black braid threaded with white shell beads, indigo captain coat, rust sash, crystal-hook staff
- Acting rule: decisive survival logic with visible maternal conflict

### Admiral Soren

- Role: guardian of the Freeze Engine and ruler of the glass routes
- Permanent design lock: tall Indian man late forties, bronze-brown skin, pale grey eyes, close-cropped salt hair, white-and-navy crystal armor, long storm cape, black glass spear
- Acting rule: formal restraint; genuinely fears the thaw more than loss of power

### Mahi

- Role: ancient human voice chosen by the living ocean
- Permanent design lock: ageless brown-skinned woman, luminous turquoise eyes, very long silver-blue hair moving like waves, layered pearl-grey sari formed from water ribbons, bare feet above the surface
- Acting rule: speaks as one person carrying many perspectives, never vague prophecy

### Lio Soren

- Role: Soren's adult daughter and elected leader of a deep settlement
- Permanent design lock: brown-skinned Indian woman age twenty-seven, steady pale-grey eyes matching Soren, cropped black curls, turquoise deep-water suit beneath woven white shawl, coral breathing collar, childhood silver bracelet carried at belt
- Acting rule: affection without surrendering authority; corrects surface assumptions directly

### Professor Kala Varma

- Role: inventor suspended inside the Freeze Engine and Nila's grandmother
- Permanent design lock: elderly dark-brown-skinned Indian woman, bright sea-green eyes, long white braid floating around face, copper engineering suit beneath translucent frost mantle, crystalline heartbeat harness across chest
- Acting rule: curious and exact despite exhaustion; refuses automatic moral authority

## Season episode spine

01. **Her Brother Knocked Beneath the Sea** — Nila hears Arun beneath a surface frozen for eighteen years.
02. **The Crack Reached the Harbor** — A rescue fracture threatens homes anchored to the frozen sea.
03. **The Admiral Knew Arun Was Alive** — Soren reveals he has monitored survivors beneath the glass for years.
04. **Her Mother Chose the Surface** — Mira explains the night she sealed Arun beneath the sea.
05. **Arun Was Breaking the Ocean Free** — Arun's sabotage forces Nila to question whether rescue can wait.
06. **The Second Engine Froze Memory** — Soren's backup machine makes citizens forget the ocean was ever liquid.
07. **Oru Turned to Glass** — Saving Oru requires returning a memory Nila prefers to keep.
08. **The Admiral's Daughter Lived Below** — Soren's personal loss exposes why he protects the prison he hates.
09. **They Walked Under the Ocean** — Nila enters the inverted world sustained beneath the frozen sea.
10. **The Ocean Asked for a Body** — The living sea chooses Nila as its messenger, threatening her individual voice.
11. **The Sea Was Dying in Its Prison** — Mahi reveals the Freeze preserved humans while suffocating ocean circulation.
12. **Fresh Water or Living Sea** — The first current test risks contaminating the surface's drinking supply.
13. **A City-Sized Whale Woke Below** — An ancient whale carries the Freeze Engine's missing counter-song.
14. **The Freeze Was Never Meant to Last** — Mira faces evidence that leaders extended the emergency to protect their new power.
15. **Exile Crossed the Singing Crack** — The exiled caravan discovers cracks can carry safe currents when people move with them.
16. **Lio Refused to Return Home** — Soren's daughter rejects rescue defined as returning to the surface.
17. **Arun Built the Shatter Key** — Arun's emergency weapon divides survivors who are running out of air.
18. **Tavi Stole the Shatter Piece** — Tavi takes the key to stop adults repeating secret decisions, but creates a new secret himself.
19. **The Frozen Tsunami Was Still Moving** — The disaster paused eighteen years ago will resume when the ocean thaws.
20. **The Engine Had a Human Heart** — The Freeze Engine is powered by the suspended consciousness of its inventor.
21. **Ten Tides to Move a Civilization** — Surface and below populations must relocate through the same limited corridors.
22. **Only Oru Could Carry the Map** — Oru must hold the changing evacuation network, risking dissolution into the ocean.
23. **The Wave Remembered Every Ship** — Thawing memory-wrecks block the final route with unresolved distress signals.
24. **Two Aruns Reached the Surface** — A sea-memory copy challenges the assumption that only the physical survivor belongs to Nila.
25. **The Ocean Cracked Everywhere** — Premature shutdown turns every evacuation into a simultaneous crisis.
26. **They Let the Tsunami Fall** — The coast faces the original wave through preparation instead of another freeze.
27. **Nila Could No Longer Skate Home** — The successful thaw destroys Nila's familiar routes and identity as a glass skater.
28. **The First Storm Had No Memory** — Eighteen years without circulation leaves the ocean unable to regulate its own weather.
29. **Kala Chose an Ordinary Life** — The inventor survives but refuses to become the permanent expert ruling the new sea.
30. **The Ocean Moved Beneath Her** — Nila releases the final shard and chooses a future between surface and deep communities.
