# ECHO//100 V2 Production Workflow

## Master-first distribution

- Primary deliverable: one 25–30 minute 16:9 film.
- Secondary deliverables: thirty 45–60 second 9:16 Shorts extracted from the master edit.
- Compilation deliverables: three 8–10 minute chapters can be cut at Episodes 10, 20, and 30 without creating new story material.
- The film and Shorts share the same performances, shots, dialogue, music, and canon.

## Monthly publishing cadence

- Publish one Short every day: approximately 30 Shorts per month from one master film.
- Publish the 25–30 minute master film once per month after final QC.
- Each Short must be a deliberately re-edited mini-story with an immediate hook, understandable conflict, meaningful turn or payoff, burned-in captions, and a clean path to the master film.
- Do not upload contextless raw extracts, duplicate cuts, identical openings, or interchangeable cliffhangers.
- Rotate focus across Kavi, Tara, Byte, Mira, Rhea, Null, the mystery rules, emotional choices, action moments, and reveals so the daily feed does not look templated.
- Shorts may change framing, pacing, captions, sound mix, opening line, and final call-forward, but may not change canon.
- Publishing automation remains private-first until technical and human QC approve the film and all derivative Shorts.

## Character-consistency gate

Required locked sheets before story-frame generation:

1. Kavi Rao
2. Tara Malik
3. Byte
4. Mira Sen
5. Dr. Rhea Rao
6. Null
7. Arvind Rao
8. Imran Malik

Every sheet must show the exact same character in front, front three-quarter, profile, back three-quarter, and back views, plus at least five expressions. Clothing, face, age, body proportions, hair, accessories, materials, palette, and scale must remain unchanged across views.

## Reference hierarchy

1. Approved individual turnaround sheet
2. Approved ensemble height/scale sheet
3. Approved expression sheet
4. Approved location/prop sheet
5. Previous approved shot from the same sequence

If a generated image conflicts with a higher reference, reject the image. Do not repair a bad identity with editing tricks.

## No-Kaggle rule

V2 does not use Kaggle, Wan benchmarking, or a remote ComfyUI tunnel. Character sheets and story images are created first. Motion generation is selected only after the still-image continuity package and long-film animatic pass QC.

## Long-film QC before Shorts

- Story causality works without captions.
- Character identity is stable in every shot.
- Dialogue voices remain stable.
- No repeated still is used as a substitute for a new dramatic beat.
- Every camera move has narrative intent.
- No generated text, logos, watermarks, malformed hands, duplicate characters, facial drift, costume drift, or unexplained prop changes.
- The 25–30 minute film passes visual, audio, subtitle, continuity, and pacing review before any Short is exported.
