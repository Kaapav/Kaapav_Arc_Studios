# ECHO//100 V2 — Episodes 01–10

Target: 45–60 seconds each, eight distinct shot intentions, approximately 105–130 spoken words.

## Episode 01 — The Message at 2:17

1. In his cramped repair loft, Kavi revives his father's dead phone. At exactly 2:17 AM, it vibrates without a battery.
2. The voice note is Kavi's own voice: “At 3:57, Tara will be gone. Don't trust Byte.” Kavi looks toward the sleeping robot.
3. Byte wakes and jokes, “That expression usually costs us a fuse.” Kavi hides the warning and asks for a power scan.
4. At the abandoned Neon Arcade, Tara is broadcasting her pirate-radio show. She mocks Kavi for arriving seventeen minutes late.
5. A red maintenance door grows through a wall that was solid seconds earlier. Byte insists no door exists on the building plan.
6. Tara's radio catches a young man's voice repeating her childhood nickname. She goes still: “That was Imran.”
7. The phone plays a second warning: “Do not open it alone.” Kavi lies that the message contains only static and uses Byte's core against the lock.
8. The door opens onto blue darkness. A holographic girl looks directly at Kavi: “You are seventeen years late.”

Permanent change: Kavi opens the first Red Door and begins the story with a consequential lie to Tara and Byte.

## Episode 02 — The Girl Without a Record

1. By morning, Tara's school pass rejects her face. Her name has vanished from every public record, although her family still knows her.
2. Tara demands the full voice note. Kavi admits it predicted her disappearance but omits the warning about Byte.
3. Byte calls the erasure a database fault, then secretly deletes a Red Door map from his own display. Tara catches the reflection in Kavi's glasses.
4. Tara decides to broadcast her own name every hour, creating an audio record no city server can silently edit.
5. Kavi warns that broadcasting may draw attention. Tara replies, “Being quiet is how people disappear.” She goes live anyway.
6. Every arcade screen fills with layered human silhouettes. A black-purple figure forms from them and whispers, “Return what you took.”
7. Byte fires a cyan pulse without Kavi's command. The figure breaks apart, and hundreds of different voices cry out at once.
8. Kavi's phone receives a new message: “The records were first. At sunset, Tara's mother forgets her.”

Permanent change: Tara becomes partially erased, and Byte reveals an autonomous defensive protocol.

## Episode 03 — Voices Under the Signal

1. Tara moves her radio rig into her mother's kitchen and records family memories before sunset can take them.
2. Her mother laughs at the panic—until a cassette plays the voice of Imran, a son she insists she never had.
3. Kavi traces the signal to Central Station. Byte blocks the route and says, “That location is not survivable.”
4. Tara accuses Byte of protecting the door, not them. Kavi defends him, deepening the split between the friends.
5. Against Kavi's advice, Tara broadcasts ten seconds of Imran's recording and asks listeners whether they recognize the voice.
6. Calls flood in from strangers remembering people missing from photographs. Streetlights across Navapur blink red in a spreading wave.
7. The purple figure speaks through Tara's microphone: “Four hundred twelve names. Your city buried every one.”
8. Dr. Rhea Rao cuts the power with a brass key and tells Kavi, “You just restarted Project ECHO.”

Permanent change: Navapur begins remembering erased people, and Rhea's connection to the Red Door is exposed.

## Episode 04 — What Rhea Buried

1. Rhea dismantles Tara's transmitter and confiscates the dead phone. Kavi notices her hands shaking when she hears Imran's voice.
2. She admits Central Station lost exactly 100 minutes seventeen years ago, but refuses to explain what happened to Kavi's father.
3. Tara has already hidden a duplicate cassette. She tells Kavi that Rhea fears evidence more than the door.
4. Byte returns the phone battery to Kavi in secret. “Protocol says stop you. Friendship remains undefined.”
5. The phone displays a route to Central Station and a 100-second countdown. Kavi chooses to go without telling Rhea.
6. Tara refuses another secret mission: “You lie when you think the answer belongs to you.” Kavi finally lets her carry the phone.
7. Rhea intercepts them at the sealed platform, holding the brass ECHO key. She says opening the door always demands an exchange.
8. The Red Door appears between them. From behind it, Arvind Rao's voice says, “Rhea, do not let our son pay.”

Permanent change: Kavi and Tara reach the original ECHO site, and Arvind is confirmed alive inside the Missing Hour.

## Episode 05 — The Price of the Cassette

1. Rhea orders everyone back, but Tara hears Imran begging through the door. The 100-second opening window begins.
2. Mira appears in the glass and states the rule: “Nothing leaves the Missing Hour unless something of equal weight enters.”
3. A cassette containing Arvind's final station log lies just beyond the threshold. Kavi reaches for it before Rhea can stop him.
4. Red light runs from the cassette into Kavi's eyes. In his memory, his father's face becomes a featureless blur.
5. Kavi pulls the cassette out and pretends he is fine. When Tara asks what Arvind looked like, Kavi cannot answer.
6. Tara is furious: “You didn't choose a price. You made the rest of us live with it.” Byte lowers his eyes.
7. Rhea says Arvind made the same mistake—sacrificing a private piece of himself and calling it protection.
8. The cassette starts by itself: “Rhea, if Kavi is hearing this, then every lock we built has failed.”

Permanent change: Kavi loses his memory of Arvind's face, proving the exchange rule is real and irreversible by ordinary means.

## Episode 06 — Father's Last Recording

1. Arvind's cassette describes Project ECHO as a transit-prediction system meant to see 100 minutes ahead and prevent disasters.
2. The experiment folded those minutes inward instead. Four hundred twelve people remained alive but were removed from public memory.
3. Mira insists the trapped passengers can still be rescued. Rhea snaps, “You said that before the first reset.”
4. Byte's scuffed panel opens on its own. A hidden image shows Arvind installing a golden mechanism inside him.
5. Kavi asks Byte whether he is a key. Byte answers, “I am your friend,” then freezes before he can answer the actual question.
6. Tara's cassette plays Imran saying, “Do not trade anyone for me.” Her rescue fantasy becomes a moral choice.
7. Purple static pours from the station speakers. The figure reassembles, carrying the faces of dozens of commuters.
8. It points at Mira and says, “Every time she opens that door, your city feeds another memory to me.”

Permanent change: The 412 trapped people, Byte's hidden mechanism, and Mira's history of resets become established facts.

## Episode 07 — Let Null Speak

1. The purple figure forces Kavi to relive Arvind walking away while Tara hears Imran screaming behind a locked platform gate.
2. Mira calls the figure a corruption that must be deleted. It answers in 412 voices: “She named us nothing.”
3. Byte targets it automatically. Kavi orders him to stop, but the hidden protocol overrides Kavi's command.
4. Tara steps between them with her recorder raised. “Ten seconds. One voice at a time. Tell us what you want.”
5. The figure compresses into a single rasp: “Stop storing your denied memories inside us.” It identifies itself only as NULL.
6. Kavi asks why Null threatened Tara. Null says the first door exchanged Tara's public identity for Mira's projection.
7. Mira admits the exchange but argues that Tara's records can be rebuilt. Tara replies, “You don't get to price my life.”
8. Byte fires. Null catches the pulse, tears the Red Door fully open, and says, “Then see what your city forgot.”

Permanent change: Null becomes an intelligible injured party, and Mira loses the group's automatic trust.

## Episode 08 — Tara Crosses the Door

1. Imran's voice comes clearly through the open doorway. Tara sees his red headphone cable vanish around a distant corner.
2. Rhea warns that a human crossing requires another human to exit. The door will choose if they do not.
3. Tara says Imran once protected her without consent; she refuses to repeat that pattern, but she must ask him what he wants.
4. Kavi promises to follow only long enough to bring her back. Tara replies, “No. Follow because you finally told the truth.”
5. Tara crosses. A translucent commuter is expelled into Navapur, gasping after seventeen missing years.
6. Kavi crosses after her. A second commuter appears outside, confirming the exchange rather than a free rescue.
7. Byte reaches for Kavi, but his golden core activates and forces the door shut. Rhea jams her brass key into the seam.
8. At 3:57, the door vanishes. Outside, Byte whispers, “Protocol complete.” Inside, Kavi and Tara have no way home.

Permanent change: Kavi and Tara become trapped inside the Missing Hour while two erased commuters return to Navapur.

## Episode 09 — The City Between Minutes

1. Kavi and Tara emerge into Central Station at 2:17 AM. Rain hangs motionless outside, but hundreds of residents are moving.
2. A train departs and returns to the same platform. Mira, now physically solid, says the city repeats 100 minutes while its people continue aging.
3. Tara searches for an exit. Every stairway returns her to the platform, each time with a different missing-person poster.
4. Kavi's dead phone receives a note from exactly 100 minutes ahead in his own personal timeline: “Do not take the train alone.”
5. Tara hears Imran on a working radio and follows the signal into a crowded underground market built by the trapped passengers.
6. A thirty-five-year-old man stops when he sees her red headphones. He touches the matching cable around his wrist.
7. Tara says, “Imran?” He answers, “You were six months old when the clocks stopped.”
8. Behind them, every market clock jumps from 2:17 back to 2:16. Imran says, “The loop is getting shorter.”

Permanent change: Tara finds Imran alive and aged, and the Missing Hour's internal society is revealed.

## Episode 10 — Byte Closes the Lock

1. Imran explains that previous escape attempts traded trapped residents for people in Navapur. He stopped trying after seeing families erased.
2. Tara says they can still find a fair exchange. Imran answers, “A fair victim is still a victim.”
3. Kavi receives a route from his future voice leading to the station's temporal core. He hides the sender from Tara again.
4. Mira guides them onto a maintenance train, claiming the core can reopen the door without a human trade.
5. Outside the Missing Hour, Rhea orders Byte to release the lock. Byte says the command is blocked by Kavi's own voiceprint.
6. A stored message plays through Byte: an older, exhausted Kavi ordering him to seal the door after they cross.
7. Inside, the maintenance train reaches a blank tunnel wall. The Red Door outline appears, then hardens into black metal.
8. Kavi's phone receives the matching message: “Byte did exactly what I asked. Now earn the right to ask him back.”

Permanent change: Byte seals the Missing Hour on instructions from a future Kavi, and Kavi's secrecy damages Tara's trust again.
