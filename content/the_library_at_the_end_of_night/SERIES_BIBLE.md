# THE LIBRARY AT THE END OF NIGHT — Series Bible

**Genre:** dark fantasy adventure
**Premise:** At the final minute before dawn, thirteen-year-old Aadi Rao finds a library containing the endings of every unfinished story—including the last page of his missing mother's life.
**Season engine:** Each borrowed ending alters the waking world and reveals how the Library protects possibility by imprisoning stories that refuse to finish.
**Ending promise:** Aadi must save his mother without assigning endings to other lives or trapping the world in permanent night.

## Non-negotiable visual identity

Vertical 9:16 premium cute cinematic 3D feature-animation, original compact stylized characters with large expressive eyes, immense dark-fantasy library, midnight indigo, candle gold, ink-black shadows, floating books and impossible stairways, tactile paper and wood, emotionally truthful acting, eerie but not graphic; preserve all locked character sheets exactly.

## Locked cast

### Aadi Rao

- Role: thirteen-year-old compulsive ending-fixer
- Permanent design lock: small Indian boy, warm brown skin, huge dark-hazel eyes, round face, thick black hair swept left, indigo school blazer over cream shirt, rust scarf, charcoal shorts over leggings, brown boots, brass bookmark compass in breast pocket
- Acting rule: reaches for solutions when scared; growth appears as willingness to wait and ask

### Sumi Das

- Role: fourteen-year-old oral storyteller who distrusts written certainty
- Permanent design lock: small Indian teen girl, deep brown skin, large amber eyes, long black hair in two looped braids, mustard hooded cape, teal dress over trousers, red boots, wooden story beads around wrist
- Acting rule: animated hands when inventing, complete stillness when listening

### Nib

- Role: book-moth custodian born from an unfinished sentence
- Permanent design lock: palm-sized plush-like cream moth, four paper-textured wings edged in ink blue, round black eyes, tiny brass reading spectacles, ribbon bookmark tail, no mouth
- Acting rule: emotion through wing folds, punctuation-shaped dust and spectacles angle

### Librarian Iyra

- Role: ageless keeper who enforces endings she no longer questions
- Permanent design lock: tall brown-skinned Indian woman appearing early thirties, luminous silver eyes, very long black hair braided with paper strips, layered midnight robe with gold shelf-key belt, ink-stained fingers
- Acting rule: formal calm gradually reveals exhaustion and buried tenderness

### Kavya Rao

- Role: Aadi's missing mother and former field librarian
- Permanent design lock: Indian woman late thirties, warm brown skin, kind dark-hazel eyes, shoulder-length wavy black hair, moss-green explorer coat, maroon scarf matching Aadi's rust tone, leather satchel of blank books
- Acting rule: curiosity with accountable caution; never merely waits to be rescued

### The First Reader

- Role: Varun, a child who used the first written ending to freeze his dying world
- Permanent design lock: slender brown-skinned boy appearing fifteen, pale gold eyes, straight black hair turning white at tips, antique ivory tunic beneath torn black scholar coat, silver quill shaped like a small blade
- Acting rule: ancient grief inside adolescent certainty; persuasive, not melodramatic

### The Library

- Role: living architecture created from every story's right to remain possible
- Permanent design lock: endless midnight library of dark carved wood, candle constellations, floating spiral shelves, paper bridges, one enormous round dawn window, no literal face
- Acting rule: responds through shelf movement, page wind, candle color and spatial choices
