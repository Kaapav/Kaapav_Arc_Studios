# ARC I — THE EXCHANGE

Production rule: six distinct five-second shots per episode, followed by a one-second story card. Dialogue is deliberately short enough for natural delivery. Every visual is a single production frame to be generated against `assets/references/character_world_master.png`; no panel is permission to create a slideshow.

## Episode 01 — The Train Returned His Missing Sister

Arin exchanges himself for Meera, believing the rescue is complete. The train reveals that he has entered a larger game.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | On the rain-soaked abandoned platform, Arin and quadruped Tick face an impossible train emerging through fog. | Narrator: “For three hundred and seventeen nights, Arin waited for the train that stole his sister.” |
| 02 | The carriage door opens. Meera appears behind wet glass, frightened rather than relieved. | Arin: “Meera?” Meera: “Don’t open the door!” |
| 03 | A blank brass exchange plaque wakes red between Arin’s hand and Meera. | Station: “One may return. One must take their place.” |

End card: **THE NEXT DOOR OPENS AT MIDNIGHT**

## Episode 02 — The Girl Who Forgot Her Name

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Arin enters a carriage lined with thousands of hanging brass name tags; each tag whispers in a different voice. | Conductor, over speaker: “Door One collects what passengers stop defending.” |

End card: **SHE HAS BEEN SIXTEEN FOR 28 YEARS**

| 02 | Tick remains quadruped and projects cyan clock marks onto the mirror; his notched ear rotates toward an unheard bell. | Meera: “You can hear the train?” |

End card: **THE CITY FORGOT AN ENTIRE PLATFORM**
