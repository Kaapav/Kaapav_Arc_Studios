# ARC II — THE ERASED CITY

Production rule: six distinct five-second shots per episode, followed by a one-second story card. This arc widens the world without abandoning the sibling story: every supernatural discovery must alter Arin, Meera, Saira, Maya, Tick, or the Conductor.

## Episode 11 — The Bridge Nobody Remembered

The four platform allies cross an erased bridge that exists only while someone actively remembers it.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | At dawn, Meera faces an uninterrupted river where the east bridge should stand; Saira’s compass points straight across open water. | Meera: “A city can’t forget forty thousand tonnes of steel.” |
| 02 | Maya rings her dented handbell. For one second, rusted bridge cables appear inside the fog, then vanish. | Maya: “Matter follows memory here. Keep speaking.” |
| 03 | Meera, Saira, Maya, and quadruped Tick step onto invisible decking, each naming a detail of the old bridge. | Saira: “Blue rails. Lion bolts. Tea seller at the centre.” |
| 04 | Halfway across, Maya forgets the bridge and falls through; Tick anchors all four paws and catches her shawl with his teeth. | Meera: “Maya—remember the bell!” |
| 05 | Saira opens her compass. Inside is a tiny 1998 photograph of the bridge and her little brother Rohan. The entire span solidifies. | Saira: “He’ll be thirty-six now.” |
| 06 | Under the far pier they find a locked station archive door bearing the same thirteen cracks as Arin’s badge. | Maya: “The train hid its accounts beneath the city.” |

End card: **EVERY LOST MEMORY LEAVES A DOOR**

## Episode 12 — Saira Came Home 28 Years Late

Saira finds her adult brother, but returning to him would expose Platform 13 before the team understands the cost.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Saira watches thirty-six-year-old Rohan lock a small bicycle shop; he wears her silver compass symbol as a faded tattoo. | Saira, whisper: “He kept looking.” |
| 02 | Rohan notices her across the road and freezes—the exact face from the missing poster he has carried for decades. | Rohan: “Saira?” |
| 03 | Saira starts toward him, but nearby street signs and faces lose detail as the memory debt wakes around her. | Maya: “If he remembers you all at once, something else disappears.” |
| 04 | Saira steps back into shadow and phones the shop instead, hearing her brother’s voice without claiming her place. | Saira: “Did your sister like trains?” Rohan: “She hated goodbyes.” |
| 05 | Rohan turns toward the empty street while Saira silently breaks; Meera takes her hand without a speech. | Rohan: “Who is this?” Saira: “Someone who found her.” |
| 06 | In the archive, Saira adds her own photograph to a board of returned passengers and writes **NOT HOME YET** beneath it. | Saira: “We end the debt first. Then I knock.” |

End card: **HOME IS NOT THE SAME AS RETURNING**

## Episode 13 — The Fire That Should Have Killed Her

The archive reveals why Saira was selected and gives Arin the rule connecting every passenger.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | The archive projects Saira’s 1998 apartment fire from a punched railway card; sixteen-year-old Saira carries young Rohan through smoke. | Saira: “I remember getting him out.” |
| 02 | A collapsing beam falls toward her, but a station clock inexplicably stops; Saira moves through the frozen second and survives. | Maya: “That second was borrowed.” |
| 03 | One week later in the memory, the Midnight Train appears on a road with no tracks and takes Saira while Rohan sleeps. | Saira: “It didn’t save me. It collected me.” |
| 04 | Inside the present train, Arin finds matching records: every passenger survived an impossible death before disappearing. | Arin: “We’re not random. We’re debts.” |
| 05 | Arin’s own card shows a blurred figure pushing him away from the tracks 317 nights ago; the identity is deliberately obscured. | Arin: “Who saved me?” |
| 06 | Nila appears behind the record glass and traces Meera’s turquoise hair clip in the blur. | Nila: “Your sister paid first.” |

End card: **MEERA WAS NEVER THE TARGET**

## Episode 14 — The Train Chose Arin

Arin learns Meera was taken because she prevented his collection, while Meera discovers the city is beginning to forget her.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | The record completes: 317 nights earlier, young Arin stands hypnotized on the track as the Midnight Train approaches. | Narrator: “The badge did not call the train for Meera.” |
| 02 | Meera tackles Arin clear and lands inside the open carriage; the door closes before he can reach her. | Meera in memory: “Don’t open the door!” |
| 03 | Present Arin confronts the Conductor in the corridor, holding the cracked badge like evidence. | Arin: “She took my place before I took hers.” |
| 04 | The Conductor’s ivory mask tilts; a human eye behind one slit is wet but controlled. | Conductor: “The train recognizes sacrifice. It does not understand love.” |
| 05 | In the city, Meera’s school registration, bedroom photos, and messages begin blanking while she watches. | Meera: “It’s charging me twice.” |
| 06 | Tick projects a countdown from the notch in his ear: **MEERA / 09 NIGHTS**. | Maya: “When it reaches zero, only we will know she existed.” |

End card: **THE CITY IS FORGETTING MEERA**

## Episode 15 — Tick’s Thirteenth Gear

Tick’s origin links Maya to the Conductor and provides the mechanism needed to preserve Meera’s identity.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Tick collapses in the archive as twelve visible clockwork gears stop; a sealed thirteenth socket glows beneath his ivory chest plate. | Meera: “He gave the station too much time.” |
| 02 | Maya opens an old tool case engraved **E.D. / 1976**; inside is a gear that matches Tick perfectly. | Saira: “Who is E.D.?” Maya: “My brother Elias.” |
| 03 | Memory fragment: teenage Elias builds the small quadruped fox for child Maya, carefully notching its right ear. | Elias in memory: “So you’re never alone at midnight.” |
| 04 | The Midnight Train comes for Maya after she survives a station roof collapse; Elias pushes her out and boards with Tick. | Young Maya: “Elias!” |
| 05 | Present Maya installs the thirteenth gear. Tick’s cyan eyes relight and project every handwritten memory of Meera stored inside him. | Maya: “He has remembered her for all of us.” |
| 06 | On the train, the Conductor feels Tick restart and grips the same E.D. engraving inside his glove. | Conductor, softly: “Good fox.” |

End card: **MAYA’S BROTHER IS STILL ON THE TRAIN**

## Episode 16 — The City Without Shadows

Nila releases stolen memories to accelerate the collapse, giving citizens glimpses of lives that never happened.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | At noon, people cross a busy square but cast no shadows; behind them appear translucent shadows of missing passengers instead. | News voice: “Authorities report a citywide optical event.” |
| 02 | Meera’s shadow is Arin inside the train; his shadow looks up and meets her gaze independently. | Arin’s shadow: “Door Seven. She opened it.” |
| 03 | Inside Door Seven, Arin and Saira enter a miniature version of the city made from thousands of bottled memories. | Saira: “Nila moved the debt out of the train.” |
| 04 | Nila uncorks a bottle labelled only by editorial text **EAST BRIDGE**; outside, the real bridge reappears violently through traffic. | Nila: “I can return everything.” |
| 05 | Another bottle opens and a living family forgets the child standing between them; Meera pulls the child to safety. | Meera: “Returning a place erased a person.” |
| 06 | Arin refuses Nila’s offered bottle containing Meera’s complete memory. | Arin: “Not if somebody else pays for her.” |

End card: **EVERY RESTORED MEMORY ERASES ANOTHER**

## Episode 17 — The Face Behind the Clock

Arin damages the Conductor’s mask during a confrontation and sees that the apparent jailer is human and aging.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Arin blocks the corridor with the 1976 passenger ledger while Saira locks the carriage behind them. | Arin: “Elias D’Souza. Passenger Forty-One.” |
| 02 | The Conductor advances without drawing a weapon; the twelve doors rotate around him like clock hands. | Conductor: “Give me that name.” |
| 03 | Nila jolts the train. Arin and the Conductor collide; the ivory clock mask cracks from brow to jaw. | Saira: “Arin—move!” |
| 04 | Through the crack is an older Goan Indian man’s face with Maya’s eyes, aged by fifty years but still recognizably Elias. | Arin: “Maya kept your gear.” |
| 05 | Elias removes one glove, revealing a passenger number burned into his palm, and stops attacking. | Elias: “Maya is alive?” |
| 06 | Before Arin can answer, black clock-light floods the corridor and Nila takes control of Door Eight. | Nila: “He chose the train over her. Ask him why.” |

End card: **THE CONDUCTOR IS MAYA’S BROTHER**

## Episode 18 — The First Exchange

Elias shows Arin the missing ending of Nila’s 1913 memory: Passenger Zero’s release would collect the memories of an entire city.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Door Eight becomes the 1913 control room as engineer Devika Rao turns the master clock back one fatal minute. | Elias: “The minute saved thirteen bodies and duplicated thirteen lives.” |
| 02 | Two versions of the station overlap catastrophically—one intact, one wrecked—while people forget which families are theirs. | Devika: “I saved them.” Station voice: “You divided them.” |
| 03 | Thirteen-year-old Nila understands first and steps onto the newborn train, clock-light cracking across her hands. | Nila: “Take my life. Leave their memories.” |
| 04 | The two stations fuse safely; Nila becomes the living lock while Devika forgets she ever had a daughter. | Elias: “The first exchange was voluntary.” |
| 05 | Over a century, the train mechanizes the sacrifice, collecting replacements and storing unpaid memories behind twelve doors. | Arin: “A promise became a prison.” |
| 06 | Elias shows what Nila omits: if she exits carrying all stored debt, every living citizen becomes a body without personal memory. | Elias: “She can free the passengers—or leave a city of strangers.” |

End card: **NILA IS NOT LYING — SHE IS DESPERATE**

## Episode 19 — The Last Person Who Remembers Meera

As Meera’s countdown expires, her allies deliberately transfer her identity into the one system the train cannot edit: freely shared memory.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Meera enters her home; her father politely asks who she is while her own photographs show an empty space beside Arin. | Father: “Can I help you?” Meera: “No. I’m sorry.” |
| 02 | At Platform 13, Maya forgets Meera mid-sentence; Saira immediately says Meera’s name and restores the recognition for one breath. | Saira: “Meera Rao. Twelve. Teal coat. Saved her brother.” |
| 03 | Tick projects hundreds of silent memories: Meera repairing his ear, laughing in rain, writing MOON on her palm. | Meera: “He can’t hold all of me alone.” |
| 04 | The twelve bridge witnesses return and each learns one true memory of Meera, accepting that they may lose something else. | Nurse: “I’ll remember the warning at the door.” |
| 05 | Countdown reaches zero. The city forgets Meera, but twelve witnesses speak their chosen memories and her outline becomes solid again. | Witnesses, overlapping: “We remember Meera.” |
| 06 | On the train, every locked door displays Meera’s turquoise hair clip symbol; Arin hears the city speak her name. | Arin: “She found a payment the train didn’t steal.” |

End card: **MEMORY CAN BE GIVEN — NOT ONLY TAKEN**

## Episode 20 — The Man Behind the Mask

Elias reveals his full history to Maya and joins the siblings’ plan, completing the arc while Nila seizes the train’s engine.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Meera uses Tick’s thirteenth gear and Maya’s handbell to open a stable mirror between Platform 13 and the Conductor’s carriage. | Meera: “Maya. Your brother is listening.” |
| 02 | Maya sees the cracked mask. Elias removes it completely, revealing the older face of the teenager who saved her in 1976. | Maya: “You missed fifty birthdays.” Elias: “I counted every one.” |
| 03 | Elias explains that he became Conductor after the previous keeper vanished, using the role to slow Nila’s releases. | Elias: “I enforced the rule because a bad lock was all we had.” |
| 04 | Maya places her palm on the mirror; Elias aligns his palm without touching, mirroring Episode 1 without an exchange. | Maya: “Then help us build a better one.” |
| 05 | Elias gives Arin the master ticket punch and names him Passenger One by choice, not ownership. | Elias: “A rule born from consent can be rewritten by consent.” |
| 06 | The engine tears open. Nila sits in the driver’s position as all twelve doors unlock toward the city. | Nila: “You had a century. I’m opening everything.” |

End card: **ARC II COMPLETE — THE TRAIN HAS NO CONDUCTOR**
