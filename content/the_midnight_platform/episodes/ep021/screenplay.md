# Episode 21 — The Train Entered the City

With Nila driving, the Midnight Train leaves its hidden railway and lays moving tracks through the waking city.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | At midnight, brass rails grow down the centre of a modern rain-wet avenue; the Midnight Train emerges between buses and buildings. | News voice: “A train is moving through the east district. There are no tracks.” |
| 02 | Nila drives barefoot, cracked hands fused to the clock controls, while stored memories orbit the engine like bottled stars. | Nila: “Every passenger gets a door home.” |
| 03 | Arin, Saira, and unmasked Elias race across the roof toward the engine as the city bends into overlapping years below. | Elias: “If the engine reaches Central, the debt reaches everyone.” |
| 04 | Meera, Maya, and quadruped Tick pursue in Maya’s tiny maintenance trolley along tracks forming seconds ahead of them. | Meera: “Then we reach Central first.” |
| 05 | One carriage door opens beside a hospital. A missing nurse steps out; across the street, a family simultaneously forgets why they are crying. | Maya: “One return. One memory taken.” |
| 06 | The train’s destination board changes in clean editorial type to **CENTRAL STATION / LAST STOP / 12:13**. | Elias: “Thirteen minutes until the city is emptied.” |

End card: **THE LAST STOP IS THE WHOLE CITY**
