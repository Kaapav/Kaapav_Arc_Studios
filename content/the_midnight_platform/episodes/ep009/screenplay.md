# Episode 09 — How to Call a Train That Does Not Exist

The platform team constructs a physical recall signal while Arin and Saira fight to reach the matching door from inside.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Maya lays out the recall mechanism: Tick’s lost tail segment, Arin’s childhood badge, Meera’s written word MOON, and her brass handbell. | Maya: “A machine needs coordinates. A ghost train needs witnesses.” |
| 02 | Meera recruits twelve ordinary night workers—porter, nurse, baker, driver—each holding a mirror and agreeing to remember Platform 13. | Meera: “If it edits one of us, the others say it back.” |
| 03 | Inside, Arin and Saira sprint through a tilting carriage as doors relabel themselves; only Arin’s cracked badge points to Door Four. | Saira: “Your sister is building a station under us.” |
| 04 | Tick drives the control gear on four paws while his remaining tail sparks; the sealed platform arch begins breathing steam. | Maya: “Hold the minute!” |
| 05 | Door Four and Platform 13 overlap for three seconds. Arin sees Meera at arm’s length, separated by transparent clock glass. | Meera: “Come home.” Arin: “Not alone.” |
| 06 | Arin pushes Saira’s silver compass through the glass to Meera as the overlap closes. | Arin: “Find the year on that compass.” |

End card: **THE TRAIN ARRIVES TOMORROW**
