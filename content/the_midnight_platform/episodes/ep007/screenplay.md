# Episode 07 — The Passenger Without a Ticket

Nila gives Arin her version of the train’s history, forcing him to question the Conductor.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Arin wakes alone in a 1913 wooden carriage; Nila sits opposite, pale clock-light visible in the cracks of her hands. | Nila: “They call me Zero so nobody asks who came first.” |
| 02 | She shows a living memory: engineer Devika Rao turning a gigantic station clock backward as a derailment approaches. | Nila: “My mother stole one minute from death.” |
| 03 | Thirteen people step safely away from the crash, but their shadows remain under the wreck; young Nila walks toward the newborn train. | Nila: “Someone had to hold the minute open.” |
| 04 | Present-day Arin sees rows of passengers frozen behind glass, each wearing a blank ticket. | Arin: “The Conductor imprisoned you.” Nila: “The rule did.” |
| 05 | The clock-masked Conductor cuts through the memory with his brass ticket punch; Nila’s carriage begins collapsing. | Conductor: “She shows truth with the ending removed.” |
| 06 | As Arin is pulled out, Nila presses thirteen hairline cracks into his badge. | Nila: “Ask him why he still wears a passenger’s coat.” |

End card: **THE CONDUCTOR WAS ONCE A PASSENGER**
