# Episode 11 — The Bridge Nobody Remembered

The four platform allies cross an erased bridge that exists only while someone actively remembers it.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | At dawn, Meera faces an uninterrupted river where the east bridge should stand; Saira’s compass points straight across open water. | Meera: “A city can’t forget forty thousand tonnes of steel.” |
| 02 | Maya rings her dented handbell. For one second, rusted bridge cables appear inside the fog, then vanish. | Maya: “Matter follows memory here. Keep speaking.” |
| 03 | Meera, Saira, Maya, and quadruped Tick step onto invisible decking, each naming a detail of the old bridge. | Saira: “Blue rails. Lion bolts. Tea seller at the centre.” |
| 04 | Halfway across, Maya forgets the bridge and falls through; Tick anchors all four paws and catches her shawl with his teeth. | Meera: “Maya—remember the bell!” |
| 05 | Saira opens her compass. Inside is a tiny 1998 photograph of the bridge and her little brother Rohan. The entire span solidifies. | Saira: “He’ll be thirty-six now.” |
| 06 | Under the far pier they find a locked station archive door bearing the same thirteen cracks as Arin’s badge. | Maya: “The train hid its accounts beneath the city.” |

End card: **EVERY LOST MEMORY LEAVES A DOOR**
