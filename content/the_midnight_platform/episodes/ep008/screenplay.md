# Episode 08 — The Night Everyone Forgot the Moon

Meera forces a second signal to the train; the resulting memory debt removes the moon from the city’s shared understanding.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Meera, Maya, and quadruped Tick align thirteen station mirrors into a ring around the brass handbell. | Maya: “One signal cost a minute. Two may cost the city.” |
| 02 | Meera rings anyway. Above them the full moon flickers like a faulty lamp while Arin appears across all mirrors. | Meera: “Arin, the route says Car Four.” |
| 03 | Arin shouts back from the train as Nila’s glowing cracks spread across the mirrored sky. | Arin: “Don’t call again. It charges everyone.” |
| 04 | The signal breaks. People across the city look up at a bright circle they can see but cannot name. | Child in street: “What is that light?” |
| 05 | Maya opens a schoolbook: every moon illustration remains, but every printed label is blank under clean editorial masking. | Maya: “A shared memory. Taken whole.” |
| 06 | Meera writes **MOON** on her palm before she forgets, then looks up and says the word to Tick. | Meera: “We remember for them.” |

End card: **THE CITY PAID ARIN’S FARE**
