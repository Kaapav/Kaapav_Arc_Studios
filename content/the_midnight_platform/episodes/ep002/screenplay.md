# Episode 02 — The Girl Who Forgot Her Name

Arin’s first door contains Saira, a passenger who has protected her name for twenty-eight years.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Arin enters a carriage lined with thousands of hanging brass name tags; each tag whispers in a different voice. | Conductor, over speaker: “Door One collects what passengers stop defending.” |
| 02 | A rust-red blur tackles Arin away from a tag reaching for his chest; reveal Saira with silver compass necklace. | Saira: “Say your name. Now.” Arin: “Arin Rao.” |
| 03 | Arin repeats his name while letters vanish from his blank ticket; Saira presses her compass to her heart. | Saira: “Again. Before the carriage eats it.” |
| 04 | A tag marked only by editorial typography **SAIRA SEN / 1998** swings behind her; Arin realizes her era. | Arin: “You’ve been here since 1998?” Saira: “It’s still 1998.” |
| 05 | The carriage lights fail in sequence. A child-sized silhouette with cracked luminous hands watches through the forest of tags. | Child whisper: “He brought me another name.” |
| 06 | Arin and Saira slam the next door shut; the brass tag in Arin’s fist now carries Meera’s fingerprint, not text. | Saira: “That was Passenger Zero.” |

End card: **SHE HAS BEEN SIXTEEN FOR 28 YEARS**
