# Episode 27 — Tick Stopped the Clock

Tick spends all thirteen of his internal minutes to hold the train still long enough for the voluntary memory network to connect.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Central Station’s master clock races toward 12:13 as the train penetrates the concourse; the cyan memory network remains incomplete. | Elias: “We need sixty seconds the train cannot own.” |
| 02 | Tick looks from Meera to his thirteenth gear and climbs the clock mechanism on four paws. | Meera: “No. We find another way.” |
| 03 | Tick nudges Meera’s turquoise clip back into her hair, then locks his segmented tail and all four paws around the escapement. | Maya: “He already chose.” |
| 04 | All thirteen gears leave Tick’s body one by one and enter the master clock; the second hand stops one mark before 12:13. | Sound: thirteen distinct mechanical heartbeats, then silence. |
| 05 | Across the frozen city, the final voluntary memory slip lights cyan and joins the network. | Arin: “The minute is ours.” |
| 06 | Tick lies still but intact—clearly a small quadruped fox, never humanoid—while Meera carries him from the gears. | Meera: “You are more than the time inside you.” |

End card: **TICK USED HIS LAST MINUTE**
