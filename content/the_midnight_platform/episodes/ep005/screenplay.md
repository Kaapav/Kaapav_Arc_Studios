# Episode 05 — Tick Lost One Minute

Tick sacrifices part of his own clockwork to send Meera’s voice into the train.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | In Platform 13’s sealed control room, Maya exposes a dead telegraph built around thirteen concentric clock gears. | Maya: “It once called every train except his.” |
| 02 | Tick places his segmented tail into the machine while remaining firmly on four paws; cyan time flows from the blue tail tip. | Meera: “Tick, stop. That gear is you.” |
| 03 | Inside the train, every carriage clock jumps backward exactly one minute; Arin hears Meera through static. | Meera, distorted: “Arin—Door Thirteen is not an exit.” |
| 04 | Passenger Zero’s cracked hand reaches through the telegraph signal and tries to pull Meera into the mirror. | Nila: “You rang my bell.” |
| 05 | Tick bites through his own tail connector; the severed blue-tipped segment locks into the machine and the hand vanishes. | Maya: “He gave the station one of his minutes.” |
| 06 | Tick staggers but stays quadruped. The telegraph prints a clean geometric route: **CAR 04 / HOME**. | Meera: “Arin heard us. We do it again.” |
