# Episode 19 — The Last Person Who Remembers Meera

As Meera’s countdown expires, her allies deliberately transfer her identity into the one system the train cannot edit: freely shared memory.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Meera enters her home; her father politely asks who she is while her own photographs show an empty space beside Arin. | Father: “Can I help you?” Meera: “No. I’m sorry.” |
| 02 | At Platform 13, Maya forgets Meera mid-sentence; Saira immediately says Meera’s name and restores the recognition for one breath. | Saira: “Meera Rao. Twelve. Teal coat. Saved her brother.” |
| 03 | Tick projects hundreds of silent memories: Meera repairing his ear, laughing in rain, writing MOON on her palm. | Meera: “He can’t hold all of me alone.” |
| 04 | The twelve bridge witnesses return and each learns one true memory of Meera, accepting that they may lose something else. | Nurse: “I’ll remember the warning at the door.” |
| 05 | Countdown reaches zero. The city forgets Meera, but twelve witnesses speak their chosen memories and her outline becomes solid again. | Witnesses, overlapping: “We remember Meera.” |
| 06 | On the train, every locked door displays Meera’s turquoise hair clip symbol; Arin hears the city speak her name. | Arin: “She found a payment the train didn’t steal.” |

End card: **MEMORY CAN BE GIVEN — NOT ONLY TAKEN**
