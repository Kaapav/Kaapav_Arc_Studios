# Episode 04 — The Carriage Where It Always Rains

Arin and Saira rescue a child from an endless storm, and Arin learns every forced rescue charges the city in memories.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Door Two opens into a flooded railway carriage under indoor thunderclouds; schoolboy Dev clings to a luggage rack above black water. | Dev: “Please—I can’t remember my mother’s face.” |
| 02 | Saira ties her compass cord around Arin as he wades into rising water; rain falls upward around the exit. | Saira: “The door wants a memory, not a hero.” |
| 03 | Arin reaches Dev while a warm vision of his mother singing appears in the water beneath him. | Mother’s echo: “Arin, come home.” |
| 04 | Arin closes his eyes and pulls Dev free; the song collapses into silent golden droplets leaving his head. | Arin: “Take the song. Not him.” |
| 05 | On Platform 13, Meera tries to hum their mother’s lullaby but both she and Maya suddenly lose the melody. | Meera: “Why can’t I finish it?” |
| 06 | Dev escapes into a safe compartment; Arin remembers his mother’s face but no longer her voice. Saira sees the cost in him. | Saira: “You didn’t pay alone. Your whole city did.” |

End card: **THE FIRST MEMORY IS GONE**
