# Episode 03 — The Platform Inside the Mirror

Meera and Tick discover the train can be seen from the city only through reflections, and meet the one adult who remembers Platform 13.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | At 11:59 PM, Meera aims a cracked station mirror across the abandoned tracks; the real platform is empty, but the reflection shows Arin’s moving train. | Meera: “Tick. He’s still on it.” |
| 02 | Tick remains quadruped and projects cyan clock marks onto the mirror; his notched ear rotates toward an unheard bell. | Meera: “You can hear the train?” |
| 03 | A dented brass handbell enters frame. Maya D’Souza, in retired stationmaster uniform and plum shawl, stands behind them. | Maya: “No. He hears what the train owes.” |
| 04 | Maya unlocks a tiled wall that city maps show as solid; behind it is a sealed arch labelled only with clean editorial text **PLATFORM 13**. | Meera: “Who erased this?” Maya: “Everyone.” |
| 05 | Maya opens her ledger: faces remain in old photographs, but their names and surrounding memories are blank. | Maya: “The train doesn’t hide people. It edits the goodbye.” |
| 06 | In the mirror, Arin turns as though he hears Meera. She rings Maya’s bell once; the reflection fractures into thirteen doors. | Meera: “Then we make it remember us.” |

End card: **THE CITY FORGOT AN ENTIRE PLATFORM**
