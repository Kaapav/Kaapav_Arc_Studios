# Episode 26 — The City Chose at Midnight

Meera asks ordinary citizens to give one memory voluntarily, turning the finale from a hero’s private sacrifice into a public decision.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Meera hijacks every station speaker while Maya displays Rohan’s cyan exchange result across public departure boards. | Meera: “The train will take whole lives unless we choose smaller losses together.” |
| 02 | Nila broadcasts the opposite offer from the engine: surrender nothing and she will return everyone by force. | Nila: “Why should the living keep everything?” |
| 03 | A nurse offers the memory of her first day in uniform; a baker offers one recipe; a porter offers the score of a childhood match. | Nurse: “I understand the cost. I consent.” |
| 04 | Hundreds refuse and step back—shown without shame—while thousands who accept write one chosen memory on blank brass slips. | Maya: “A choice is not consent if no is forbidden.” |
| 05 | The slips glow cyan across the city, each tiny contribution flowing toward Central Station without erasing any person. | Elias: “The debt is dividing.” |
| 06 | Nila sees the engine’s red balance fall from one life to thousands of freely given memories. | Meera: “Nobody carries it alone again.” |

End card: **THE CITY SAID YES — FREELY**
