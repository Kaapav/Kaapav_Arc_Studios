# Episode 16 — The City Without Shadows

Nila releases stolen memories to accelerate the collapse, giving citizens glimpses of lives that never happened.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | At noon, people cross a busy square but cast no shadows; behind them appear translucent shadows of missing passengers instead. | News voice: “Authorities report a citywide optical event.” |
| 02 | Meera’s shadow is Arin inside the train; his shadow looks up and meets her gaze independently. | Arin’s shadow: “Door Seven. She opened it.” |
| 03 | Inside Door Seven, Arin and Saira enter a miniature version of the city made from thousands of bottled memories. | Saira: “Nila moved the debt out of the train.” |
| 04 | Nila uncorks a bottle labelled only by editorial text **EAST BRIDGE**; outside, the real bridge reappears violently through traffic. | Nila: “I can return everything.” |
| 05 | Another bottle opens and a living family forgets the child standing between them; Meera pulls the child to safety. | Meera: “Returning a place erased a person.” |
| 06 | Arin refuses Nila’s offered bottle containing Meera’s complete memory. | Arin: “Not if somebody else pays for her.” |

End card: **EVERY RESTORED MEMORY ERASES ANOTHER**
