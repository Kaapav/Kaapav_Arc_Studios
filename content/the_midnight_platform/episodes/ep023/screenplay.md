# Episode 23 — Passenger Zero Came Home

Nila reaches the modern site of her childhood house and discovers freedom cannot restore the mother who forgot her in 1913.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Nila steps off the train at a glass tower built where her 1913 railway cottage once stood. | Nila: “This is the address.” |
| 02 | Through clock-light she overlays the old cottage on the lobby; Devika sets a dinner place but cannot see her daughter. | Nila: “Amma, I came back.” |
| 03 | The memory repeats Devika asking why there is an extra plate, confirming Nila was erased the moment she boarded. | Devika’s echo: “I have no daughter.” |
| 04 | Meera approaches without a weapon and places her turquoise clip beside Nila’s bare foot as proof of a remembered person. | Meera: “Being remembered by force is not coming home.” |
| 05 | Nila’s grief sends a shock through the engine; citizens nearby lose their most cherished memories and become emotionally blank. | Nila: “Then nobody gets to keep what I lost.” |
| 06 | Arin reaches Nila but cannot touch her through the storm of stolen memories. | Arin: “I know what it is to trade yourself for a sister.” |

End card: **THE VILLAIN WAS A CHILD WHO CAME HOME TOO LATE**
