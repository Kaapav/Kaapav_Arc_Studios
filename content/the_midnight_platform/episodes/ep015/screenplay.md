# Episode 15 — Tick’s Thirteenth Gear

Tick’s origin links Maya to the Conductor and provides the mechanism needed to preserve Meera’s identity.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Tick collapses in the archive as twelve visible clockwork gears stop; a sealed thirteenth socket glows beneath his ivory chest plate. | Meera: “He gave the station too much time.” |
| 02 | Maya opens an old tool case engraved **E.D. / 1976**; inside is a gear that matches Tick perfectly. | Saira: “Who is E.D.?” Maya: “My brother Elias.” |
| 03 | Memory fragment: teenage Elias builds the small quadruped fox for child Maya, carefully notching its right ear. | Elias in memory: “So you’re never alone at midnight.” |
| 04 | The Midnight Train comes for Maya after she survives a station roof collapse; Elias pushes her out and boards with Tick. | Young Maya: “Elias!” |
| 05 | Present Maya installs the thirteenth gear. Tick’s cyan eyes relight and project every handwritten memory of Meera stored inside him. | Maya: “He has remembered her for all of us.” |
| 06 | On the train, the Conductor feels Tick restart and grips the same E.D. engraving inside his glove. | Conductor, softly: “Good fox.” |

End card: **MAYA’S BROTHER IS STILL ON THE TRAIN**
