# Episode 10 — Platform 13

The siblings reopen the lost platform and free Saira, but the city loses the memory of the bridge that connects its two halves.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Midnight. Twelve witnesses hold mirrors as Platform 13 physically unfolds from the tiled wall; the train arrives without headlights. | Maya: “Whatever steps off, keep saying its name.” |
