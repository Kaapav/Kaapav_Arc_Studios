# Episode 06 — The Door That Looked Like Home

The train offers Arin a flawless life in which Meera was never taken, but Saira spots the emotional lie.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Door Three opens onto Arin’s warm family kitchen at sunrise; his mother cooks while Meera laughs, wearing her locked teal coat. | Mother: “Breakfast before school.” |
| 02 | Arin drops his ticket and runs to them; his mother embraces him with convincing warmth. | Arin: “I remember you.” |
| 03 | Saira stays at the threshold. In a steel kettle reflection, the room is an empty carriage and the family have no faces. | Saira: “Ask Meera what she warned you.” |
| 04 | Arin asks. The smiling Meera repeats the wrong line while the room subtly resets to the same gesture. | False Meera: “I said, come inside.” |
| 05 | Arin tears his brass station badge from the vision; the kitchen peels away into paper-thin clockwork panels. | Arin: “My sister told me not to open the door.” |
| 06 | The false mother becomes Nila for one quiet second, not monstrous—only hurt. | Nila: “I only built the home you wanted.” |
