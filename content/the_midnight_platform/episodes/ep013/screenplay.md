# Episode 13 — The Fire That Should Have Killed Her

The archive reveals why Saira was selected and gives Arin the rule connecting every passenger.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | The archive projects Saira’s 1998 apartment fire from a punched railway card; sixteen-year-old Saira carries young Rohan through smoke. | Saira: “I remember getting him out.” |
| 02 | A collapsing beam falls toward her, but a station clock inexplicably stops; Saira moves through the frozen second and survives. | Maya: “That second was borrowed.” |
| 03 | One week later in the memory, the Midnight Train appears on a road with no tracks and takes Saira while Rohan sleeps. | Saira: “It didn’t save me. It collected me.” |
| 04 | Inside the present train, Arin finds matching records: every passenger survived an impossible death before disappearing. | Arin: “We’re not random. We’re debts.” |
| 05 | Arin’s own card shows a blurred figure pushing him away from the tracks 317 nights ago; the identity is deliberately obscured. | Arin: “Who saved me?” |
| 06 | Nila appears behind the record glass and traces Meera’s turquoise hair clip in the blur. | Nila: “Your sister paid first.” |

End card: **MEERA WAS NEVER THE TARGET**
