# Episode 17 — The Face Behind the Clock

Arin damages the Conductor’s mask during a confrontation and sees that the apparent jailer is human and aging.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Arin blocks the corridor with the 1976 passenger ledger while Saira locks the carriage behind them. | Arin: “Elias D’Souza. Passenger Forty-One.” |
| 02 | The Conductor advances without drawing a weapon; the twelve doors rotate around him like clock hands. | Conductor: “Give me that name.” |
| 03 | Nila jolts the train. Arin and the Conductor collide; the ivory clock mask cracks from brow to jaw. | Saira: “Arin—move!” |
| 04 | Through the crack is an older Goan Indian man’s face with Maya’s eyes, aged by fifty years but still recognizably Elias. | Arin: “Maya kept your gear.” |
| 05 | Elias removes one glove, revealing a passenger number burned into his palm, and stops attacking. | Elias: “Maya is alive?” |
| 06 | Before Arin can answer, black clock-light floods the corridor and Nila takes control of Door Eight. | Nila: “He chose the train over her. Ask him why.” |

End card: **THE CONDUCTOR IS MAYA’S BROTHER**
