# Episode 18 — The First Exchange

Elias shows Arin the missing ending of Nila’s 1913 memory: Passenger Zero’s release would collect the memories of an entire city.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Door Eight becomes the 1913 control room as engineer Devika Rao turns the master clock back one fatal minute. | Elias: “The minute saved thirteen bodies and duplicated thirteen lives.” |
| 02 | Two versions of the station overlap catastrophically—one intact, one wrecked—while people forget which families are theirs. | Devika: “I saved them.” Station voice: “You divided them.” |
| 03 | Thirteen-year-old Nila understands first and steps onto the newborn train, clock-light cracking across her hands. | Nila: “Take my life. Leave their memories.” |
| 04 | The two stations fuse safely; Nila becomes the living lock while Devika forgets she ever had a daughter. | Elias: “The first exchange was voluntary.” |
| 05 | Over a century, the train mechanizes the sacrifice, collecting replacements and storing unpaid memories behind twelve doors. | Arin: “A promise became a prison.” |
| 06 | Elias shows what Nila omits: if she exits carrying all stored debt, every living citizen becomes a body without personal memory. | Elias: “She can free the passengers—or leave a city of strangers.” |

End card: **NILA IS NOT LYING — SHE IS DESPERATE**
