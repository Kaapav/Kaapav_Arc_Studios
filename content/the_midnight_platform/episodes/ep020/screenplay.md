# Episode 20 — The Man Behind the Mask

Elias reveals his full history to Maya and joins the siblings’ plan, completing the arc while Nila seizes the train’s engine.

| Shot | Locked visual event | Dialogue / audio |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Meera uses Tick’s thirteenth gear and Maya’s handbell to open a stable mirror between Platform 13 and the Conductor’s carriage. | Meera: “Maya. Your brother is listening.” |
| 02 | Maya sees the cracked mask. Elias removes it completely, revealing the older face of the teenager who saved her in 1976. | Maya: “You missed fifty birthdays.” Elias: “I counted every one.” |
| 03 | Elias explains that he became Conductor after the previous keeper vanished, using the role to slow Nila’s releases. | Elias: “I enforced the rule because a bad lock was all we had.” |
| 04 | Maya places her palm on the mirror; Elias aligns his palm without touching, mirroring Episode 1 without an exchange. | Maya: “Then help us build a better one.” |
| 05 | Elias gives Arin the master ticket punch and names him Passenger One by choice, not ownership. | Elias: “A rule born from consent can be rewritten by consent.” |
| 06 | The engine tears open. Nila sits in the driver’s position as all twelve doors unlock toward the city. | Nila: “You had a century. I’m opening everything.” |
