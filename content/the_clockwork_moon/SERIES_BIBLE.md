# THE CLOCKWORK MOON — Series Bible

**Genre:** fantasy adventure
**Premise:** In the mountain kingdom of Veyra, thirteen-year-old apprentice clockmaker Anya discovers that the moon is an ancient machine—and every night someone is stealing one minute from it.
**Season engine:** Each stolen minute creates a physical time-fault in Veyra; repairing it reveals who built the Moon Engine, why Anya can hear its gears, and what the royal family erased from history.
**Ending promise:** Anya must repair time without restoring the unjust system the Moon Engine was designed to protect.

## Non-negotiable visual identity

Vertical 9:16 premium cute cinematic 3D feature-animation, original compact stylized characters with large expressive eyes, tactile brass clockwork, moon-silver magic and jewel-toned mountain environments, warm candlelight against cool lunar glow, coherent anatomy and props, emotionally truthful acting, adventurous rather than violent; preserve all locked character sheets exactly.

## Locked cast

### Anya Vale

- Role: thirteen-year-old apprentice clockmaker
- Permanent design lock: small brown-skinned mountain girl, huge dark amber eyes, round freckled face, thick black hair in one messy side braid tied with copper wire, teal wool coat over cream shirt, burgundy tool apron, brown leggings, brass-toed boots, crescent-shaped hearing lens over left ear
- Acting rule: listens before acting; courage appears as careful hands becoming steady

### Pip

- Role: fox-shaped automaton and lost Moon Engine key
- Permanent design lock: tiny brass-and-ivory clockwork fox, cyan glass eyes, one copper ear and one silver ear, segmented tail with seven plates, visible heart gear behind round chest window
- Acting rule: precise fox movement; emotion through ear angle, tail rhythm and heart-gear speed

### Rian Sol

- Role: fourteen-year-old runaway prince and sky-map reader
- Permanent design lock: slender brown-skinned boy, large grey-green eyes, soft black curls, midnight-blue short cape with gold star stitching, white high-collar shirt, charcoal trousers, worn red climbing boots, broken crown pin used as compass
- Acting rule: trained politeness cracks into dry humor; steps between danger and others before admitting fear

### Master Mara Vale

- Role: Anya's grandmother, royal clockmaker and keeper of a dangerous secret
- Permanent design lock: short elderly brown-skinned woman, sharp warm eyes, silver hair in a braided bun, plum mechanic robe, leather tool belt, round magnifying spectacles, left hand made of elegant brass fingers
- Acting rule: love expressed through teaching and preparation, never helpless sentiment

### Selene

- Role: ageless child custodian of the Moon Engine
- Permanent design lock: small moon-pale girl, luminous violet eyes, waist-length white hair floating as if underwater, layered silver-blue robe with gear-shaped hems, bare feet leaving tiny constellations
- Acting rule: ancient patience inside childlike stillness; wonder returns slowly

### Regent Vale

- Role: Anya's estranged father and ruler preserving the stolen timeline
- Permanent design lock: tall brown-skinned man early forties, severe amber eyes matching Anya, straight black hair streaked silver at temples, black-and-gold royal coat with mechanical shoulder mantle, moonstone signet glove
- Acting rule: controlled and persuasive; fear of losing Anya leaks through only in his hands

### Orlo

- Role: giant clockbird who carries the moon's counterweight
- Permanent design lock: large friendly owl-like clockwork bird, layered bronze feathers, round moon-white eyes, cobalt enamel wing tips, hanging silver counterweight beneath chest
- Acting rule: majestic weight with gentle curiosity; wing beats always feel mechanical and powerful

### Queen Elara

- Role: Anya's missing mother and the last human Moon Winder
- Permanent design lock: brown-skinned woman late thirties, luminous amber eyes, very long black braid threaded with moon-silver wire, ivory-and-cobalt mechanic gown, brass forearm guards, crescent scar across right palm
- Acting rule: exhausted physical discipline with immediate maternal warmth; never behaves like a mystical prize

## Season episode spine

01. **The Moon Lost a Minute** — Anya hears the moon skip and finds one impossible silver gear in her workshop.
02. **Midnight Struck Twice** — A duplicated midnight traps the market in a repeating disaster.
03. **The Prince Stole Her Map** — Rian steals Pip's moon map to expose his father's hidden gear vault.
04. **Her Father Ordered the Theft** — Anya discovers Regent Vale is her father and watches him steal a minute to save a village.
05. **The Regent Offered Her Tomorrow** — Vale offers Anya a future without disasters if she helps him steal more time.
06. **The City Hunted Its Clockmaker** — Branded a thief, Anya must repair a time-fault without letting frightened citizens identify her.
07. **Her Mother Left a Door in Time** — The moon-key opens a workshop belonging to Anya's vanished mother.
08. **The Moon Called Her Name** — A message from inside the moon sends Anya toward the sleeping clockbird Orlo.
09. **The Clockbird Chose the Ground** — Orlo refuses to abandon endangered workers, forcing Anya to choose rescue over reaching the moon.
10. **The Eclipse Needed Thirteen Minutes** — Vale attempts a thirteen-minute freeze that could stop every disaster—and stop the kingdom forever.
11. **The Moon Began to Fall** — With the engine misaligned, falling lunar debris forces enemies into a temporary alliance.
12. **The Girl Between Seconds** — Selene claims the moon was built as a prison, not a clock.
13. **Sunrise Came From Below** — The waking Dawn turns shadows into possible futures that attack their owners.
14. **The Prince's Shadow Took the Crown** — Rian's feared future seizes the palace and offers the city efficient rule without uncertainty.
15. **The Queen Was Turning the Moon** — Anya reaches Elara but discovers her mother cannot stop winding without killing Selene.
16. **Vale Built Another Pip** — Vale's hidden raven automaton offers a faster repair at the cost of Pip's identity.
17. **The Key That Served the Dawn** — A serpent key sabotages the repairs because freedom for the Dawn means freedom for all constructs.
18. **One Minute Became a Century** — Anya enters the stretched minute and returns older in memory but unchanged in body.
19. **The Perfect Repair Was Cruel** — Anya rejects the only proven solution because it would consume every clockwork key.
20. **The Dawn Told the Truth** — The Dawn reveals Veyra stole its light, but its truthful grievance does not excuse destroying descendants.
21. **Thirteen Minutes Returned at Once** — Returned minutes unleash years of postponed consequences across Veyra.
22. **Anya Was Born in a Stolen Minute** — Anya learns Vale froze her birth to save her life, binding her heartbeat to the Moon Engine.
23. **The Clockmaker Went Silent** — Without her gift, Anya must lead repairs by trusting skills and people she once treated as extensions of herself.
24. **Coil Refused the Final Socket** — Anya must respect Coil's refusal even as the moon descends toward the city.
25. **Selene Began to Break** — Freeing Selene releases dangerous light, forcing her to decide what parts of the Dawn she wants to remain.
26. **The Moon Opened Like a Door** — The open engine reveals the original agreement Veyra must renew—or reject forever.
27. **Vale Tried to Take the Burden** — Vale attempts to imprison himself as the new Winder, mistaking punishment for repair.
28. **The Kingdom Owed One Story** — Competing stories expose who still believes they own the moon's bargain.
29. **Anya Gave Away a Century** — Anya shares the lonely century she lived inside one minute and finally lets others understand its cost.
30. **They Wound the Moon Together** — Every character contributes one reversible choice to complete the new Moon Engine.
